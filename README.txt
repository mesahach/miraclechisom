miraclechisom.com: website files

HOW TO PUBLISH
1. Upload everything in this folder to the public folder of your hosting (often "public_html").
2. index.html is the homepage. work-with-me.html is the second page.
3. Point the domain miraclechisom.com at that hosting.
4. Submit https://miraclechisom.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.

HOW TO EDIT
- Text: open the .html files in any text editor.
- Colours and layout: css/styles.css (colours are at the top).
- Images: images/ (books are in images/books/).
- WhatsApp number: search for 2349061327122 and replace it.
- Book count: search for 50+ in index.html.
